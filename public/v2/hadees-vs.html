BOOK 2 OF 97 SAHIH AL-BUKHARI 51 AHADITH

Book of Belief (Kitab al-Iman)

Defining the reality of faith, its pillars, branches, and its manifestation through word and righteous action.

كِتَابُ الإِيمَانِ
Reference range: Hadith 8 – 58
Bab 1 • Bab Al-Iman
Chapter: The statement of the Prophet (ﷺ) "Islam is built on five pillars"
بَابُ قَوْلِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ: «بُنِيَ الإِسْلاَمُ عَلَى خَمْسٍ»
8
Sahih Book 2, Hadith 1 USC-MSA Ref: Vol. 1, Book 2, Hadith 8
Isnad: Al-Makki ibn Ibrahim ← Yazid ibn Abi 'Ubayd ← Salamah ibn al-Akwa' ← 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رضي الله عنهما)
حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُوسَى، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا حَنْظَلَةُ بْنُ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ بْنِ خَالِدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ:
«بُنِيَ الإِسْلاَمُ عَلَى خَمْسٍ: شَهَادَةِ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ، وَإِقَامِ الصَّلاَةِ، وَإِيتَاءِ الزَّكَاةِ، وَالْحَجِّ، وَصَوْمِ رَمَضَانَ»
Narrated 'Abdullah bin 'Umar (may Allah be pleased with them both):
Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said: "Islam is built on five (pillars):
  1. To testify that none has the right to be worshipped but Allah and Muhammad is Allah's Messenger.
  2. To offer the (compulsory congregational) prayers dutifully and perfectly.
  3. To pay Zakat (obligatory charity).
  4. To perform Hajj (i.e. Pilgrimage to Mecca).
  5. To observe fast during the month of Ramadan."
Sharh Fath al-Bari — Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani (رحمه الله)

Imam Ibn Hajar notes that Imam al-Bukhari deliberately placed this Hadith at the beginning of Kitab al-Iman to establish that Islam and Iman are interconnected realities. The metaphor of a house built on five structural pillars illustrates that while these five form the essential foundation, the structure is perfected by secondary deeds of righteousness.

Bab 2 • Branches of Faith
Chapter: Matters pertaining to Faith (Shu'ab al-Iman)
بَابُ أُمُورِ الإِيمَانِ
9
Sahih Book 2, Hadith 2 USC-MSA Ref: Vol. 1, Book 2, Hadith 9
Isnad: Qutaybah ← Al-Layth ← 'Uqayl ← Ibn Shihab ← Abu Salama ← Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه)
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالَ:
«الإِيمَانُ بِضْعٌ وَسِتُّونَ شُعْبَةً، وَالْحَيَاءُ شُعْبَةٌ مِنَ الإِيمَانِ»
Narrated Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him):
The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Faith (Iman) has over sixty branches, and Haya' (self-respect, modesty, and modesty before Allah) is a branch of faith."
Commentary Note

Haya' is specifically highlighted because it acts as an inner moral compass that prompts a believer to fulfill obligations and refrain from transgression.

13
Sahih Book 2, Hadith 6 USC-MSA Ref: Vol. 1, Book 2, Hadith 13
Isnad: Musaddad ← Yahya ← Shu'bah ← Qatadah ← Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه)
عَنْ أَنَسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالَ:
«لاَ يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ»
Narrated Anas (may Allah be pleased with him):
The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother (or neighbor) what he loves for himself."
Commentary Note

True perfection of faith requires genuine altruism and wishing well-being, guidance, and success for others just as one desires for oneself.
