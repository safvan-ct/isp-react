Primary Textual Evidences Cited in Lecture
Quranic Proof: Hukm Taklifi Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:1)
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ أَوْفُوا۟ بِٱلْعُقُودِ
"O you who have believed, fulfill all contracts/obligations."
Usul Principle: An imperative verb ('Awfoo') in divine speech signifies decisive obligation (Wajib) unless accompanied by contradicting evidence.
Hadith Proof: Hukm Wad'i (Condition) Sahih Muslim #8
«لاَ يَقْبَلُ اللَّهُ صَلاَةَ أَحَدِكُمْ إِذَا أَحْدَثَ حَتَّى يَتَوَضَّأَ»
The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Allah does not accept the prayer of any one of you if he loses ritual purity until he performs ablution (Wudu')."
Declaratory Ruling (Hukm Wad'i): Wudu' is made a legal prerequisite/condition (Shart) for the validity of Prayer.
