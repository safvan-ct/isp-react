SURAH 18 MECCAN • 110 VAYAS JUZ 15 & 16

Surah Al-Kahf

"The Cave" — Protection from fitnah and light between two Fridays.

سُورَةُ الكَهۡفِ
Reciter: Mishary Rashid Alafasy
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful.
18:1
Juz 15 Page 293
ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبْدِهِ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ وَلَمْ يَجْعَل لَّهُۥ عِوَجَا ۜ
Al-hamdu lillaahil ladheee anzala 'alaa 'abdihil kitaaba wa lam yaj'al lahoo 'iwajaa
[All] praise is due to Allah, who has sent down upon His Servant the Book and has not made therein any deviance.
Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Excerpt)

Praise be to Allah for revealing this magnificent Book to His Messenger Muhammad (ﷺ). The word 'Iwaj' means crookedness, deviation, or contradiction. Allah made this Quran upright, clear, and perfectly balanced, acting as a guidance for all of humanity.

18:2
Juz 15
قَيِّمً۬ا لِّيُنذِرَ بَأْسً۬ا شَدِيدً۬ا مِّن لَّدُنْهُ وَيُبَشِّرَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعْمَلُونَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمْ أَجْرًا حَسَنً۬ا
Qayyimal liyundhira ba'san shadeedam mil ladunhu wa yubashshiral mu'mineenal ladheena ya'maloonas saalihaati anna lahum ajran hasana
[He has made it] straight, to warn of severe punishment from Him and to give good tidings to the believers who do righteous deeds that they will have a good reward.
Tafsir Ibn Kathir

The word 'Qayyiman' means straight and guidance for mankind. It warns those who disbelieve of a severe torment, while promising believers a great and enduring reward (Paradise).

18:3
Juz 15
مَّـٰكِثِينَ فِيهِ أَبَدً۬ا
Maakitheena feehi abadaa
In which they will remain forever.
Brief Commentary

They will abide in eternal bliss in Paradise, never leaving or experiencing cessation of joy.
