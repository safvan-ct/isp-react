Course Description & Objectives

The science of Usul al-Fiqh (Principles of Islamic Jurisprudence) serves as the engine of Islamic law. It defines how textual evidences (the Quran and Sunnah) are systematically interpreted, weighed, and applied to real-world scenarios across centuries.

In this flagship 12-week program, students will analyze classical legal epistemologies, legal capacity (Ahlizyyah), linguistic commands and prohibitions (Amr & Nahy), consensus (Ijma'), analogical reasoning (Qiyas), and the five universal legal maxims (Al-Qawa'id al-Fiqhiyyah al-Kubra).

What You Will Master

Textual Deduction (Istinbat)
Understand how jurists derive specific Ahkam (Haram, Wajib, Mandub) from divine texts.
Primary & Secondary Evidences
Differentiate between Quran, Sunnah, Mutawatir, Ahad, Ijma', and Qiyas logic.
5 Universal Legal Maxims
Study core maxims such as "Certainty is not overruled by doubt" and "Hardship brings ease".
Linguistic Nuances of Shariah
Master general ('Aam), specific (Khas), absolute (Mutlaq), and qualified (Muqayyad) expressions.
